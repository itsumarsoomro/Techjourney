 RoomViewer Platform

        
The University was about to sign for a third-party room platform costing ~£40,000 every year. Instead, I designed and built RoomViewer from the ground up — React, TypeScript and React Query on the front, .NET APIs and SQL Server behind — turning a wall of identical green bars into a filterable, searchable scheduling platform now used across the organisation.

        
~£40,000/yr cost avoided — in-house build replaced the proposed third-party product outright

        
Multiple availability views, personalised pinned layouts and role-based access control

        
Self-service administration — operational teams manage it themselves, no tickets to tech support

 Timetabling Integration — 100% Reliability

        
The Azure Logic Apps integration feeding timetables to students, staff and contractors failed 4–5 times a week, choking on ~50MB payloads. I replaced it with a .NET 9 + Dapper service: retry mechanisms, structured logging, automated email alerts, daily health monitoring and annual academic rollover built in.

        
From 4–5 failures a week with manual firefighting to 100% successful execution

        
Institutional scheduling data people finally trust
